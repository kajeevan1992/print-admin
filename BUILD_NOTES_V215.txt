# v215 — pricing cost breakdown foundation

Scope: internal pricing engine only. No storefront redesign, no orders workflow, no public API expansion.

Added:
- First real pricing cost breakdown layer.
- New helper: `src/core/catalog/pricing-cost-breakdown.ts`.
- Pricing calculation now includes `costBreakdown` alongside `quoteInput` and `productionEstimate`.
- New internal endpoint:
  - `GET /api/internal/catalog/pricing-cost-breakdown?productId=<slug-or-id>&quantity=100`
  - `POST /api/internal/catalog/pricing-cost-breakdown`

What it calculates:
- base product price
- setup costs
- run/unit costs
- material/finish/print/turnaround-style line classification
- sheet/board/roll production quantity basis
- source units required
- impressions
- minimum charges
- optional product margin percent if configured

Still not final commercial pricing:
- VAT is not added yet.
- Delivery is not added yet.
- Discounts/promotions are not added yet.
- Customer/trade price bands are not added yet.
- Full machine speed/labour scheduling is not added yet.

Test:
