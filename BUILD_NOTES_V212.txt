v212 pricing quote input bridge

Scope:
- Adds the first bridge between product configuration and the future pricing engine.
- Does NOT calculate final prices yet.
- Does NOT change orders, storefront checkout, or pricing engine rules.

Added:
- src/core/catalog/pricing-quote-input.ts
- app/api/internal/catalog/pricing-quote-input/route.ts

New internal API:
- GET  /api/internal/catalog/pricing-quote-input?productId=<id-or-slug>
- POST /api/internal/catalog/pricing-quote-input

POST body example:
{
  "productId": "standard-business-cards",
  "selections": {
    "size": "85x55",
    "material": "350gsm-silk",
    "finish": "matt-lam",
    "quantity": 500,
    "turnaround": "standard"
  }
}

Returns:
- normalized product pricing inputs
- selected option/value mapping
- setup/run/minimum charge inputs where configured
- production codes
- missing role warnings
- pricingEngineStatus: input-ready | needs-configuration

Test after deploy:
1. Open a product in Products.
2. Confirm option groups have pricing roles for size/material/quantity.
3. Visit /api/internal/catalog/pricing-input-report and confirm product readiness.
4. Visit /api/internal/catalog/pricing-quote-input?productId=<created-product-id-or-slug>.
5. POST sample selections to confirm normalized quote input payload is returned.
