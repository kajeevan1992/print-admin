# v281 FIXED — VAT + Materials + Pricing Source Foundation

Base: v280 plugin parity/preflight gate ZIP.

Scope applied only from confirmed requirements:
- Line-item VAT foundation: product/add-on VAT, mixed VAT basket, zero-rated leaflets/booklets and standard-rated business cards/boards/design services.
- Material physical specs: sheet, board and roll stock records with sizes/roll lengths and compatible machine keys.
- Machine capability foundation: sheet/board/roll capability, max widths/sizes and variable roll length support.
- Pricing source selector foundation: internal engine, supplier API and Excel/matrix upload.
- Trade supplier foundation: suppliers, credentials placeholder, synced products/options/prices model, clone/edit/hide/block/markup controls.

New internal routes:
- GET/POST /api/internal/catalog/vat-engine
- GET/POST /api/internal/catalog/material-specs
- GET/POST /api/internal/catalog/machine-capabilities
- GET/POST /api/internal/catalog/pricing-sources
- GET/POST /api/internal/catalog/trade-suppliers

Notes:
- VAT is not global. VAT must be calculated per invoice/order line.
- Design services are standard VAT even when the base product is zero-rated.
- No live supplier API calls yet; this is the registry/data-shape foundation for v282.
- No pricing engine logic was modified.
- No payment gateway or live money movement was added.
