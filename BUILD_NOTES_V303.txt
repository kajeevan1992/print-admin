V303 — Cart & Checkout Theme Bridge

Changed files:
- app/api/internal/storefront/cart/route.ts
- app/api/internal/storefront/checkout/route.ts
- src/core/storefront/cart-checkout-bridge.ts
- BUILD_NOTES_V303.txt

What changed:
- Added hosted-theme internal cart bridge at /api/internal/storefront/cart.
- Added hosted-theme internal checkout bridge at /api/internal/storefront/checkout.
- Cart supports add item, update quantity/options, remove item, clear cart, and automatic pricing recalculation.
- Checkout converts cart into the existing quote draft orders storage key without requiring artwork yet. Artwork/preflight bridge is reserved for v304.
- VAT is calculated per line item, with zero VAT for leaflet/booklet product classes and standard VAT for business cards, boards, banners, and add-on services.
- Add-ons are calculated as separate VAT-rated lines so design services remain standard VAT even when the base product is zero-rated.
- Pricing source is carried through on every cart item and draft order payload.
- Delivery estimate returns a basic working-day estimate from selected turnaround.

Test routes:
- GET /api/internal/storefront/cart
- POST /api/internal/storefront/cart
- PUT /api/internal/storefront/cart
- DELETE /api/internal/storefront/cart?id=<cartItemId>
- DELETE /api/internal/storefront/cart?clear=true
- GET /api/internal/storefront/checkout
- POST /api/internal/storefront/checkout
