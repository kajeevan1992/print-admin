v218 — Pricing diagnostics checkpoint

Purpose
- Stop adding deeper pricing formulas until the current pricing chain can be inspected clearly.
- Add a diagnostic endpoint that explains whether a product is ready for commercial pricing and why not.

Added
- src/core/catalog/pricing-diagnostics.ts
- app/api/internal/catalog/pricing-diagnostics/route.ts

New endpoint
- GET  /api/internal/catalog/pricing-diagnostics?productId=<id-or-slug>&quantity=100
- POST /api/internal/catalog/pricing-diagnostics

POST body example
{
  "productId": "standard-business-cards",
  "quantity": 100,
  "selections": {
    "size": "85x55",
    "material": "300gsm-silk",
    "finish": "matt-lamination"
  }
}

What it checks
- Required pricing roles such as quantity.
- Whether commercial roles exist: size/material/finish/turnaround/etc.
- Whether customer selections resolved cleanly or used fallbacks.
- Whether sheet/roll/board production estimate can calculate source units.
- Whether chargeable cost lines exist.
- Whether pricing rules/adjustments are being applied.
- Whether final sell price is greater than zero.

Deploy/test notes
1. Deploy as normal.
2. Test an existing product:
   /api/internal/catalog/pricing-diagnostics?productId=<your-product-slug>&quantity=100
3. Expect status:
   - ready: product has enough pricing data
   - warnings: usable but still needs configuration
   - blocked: missing required commercial pricing data

No UI redesign. No storefront changes. No order flow changes.
