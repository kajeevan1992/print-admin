- Tags create/update/delete now calls /api/internal/catalog/tags.
- Tag published/sidebar toggles now persist through the internal API.

- Module audit route now reports v187 and includes Collections/Tags as connected.

Test after deploy:
1. Open /collections.
2. Confirm DB/API status appears.
3. Create a collection and select the product/category you created earlier.
4. Refresh /collections and confirm it remains.
5. Open /api/internal/catalog/collections and confirm the collection exists with metadataJson.productIds/categoryIds.
6. Open /tags.
7. Create a tag with friendly URL, published/sidebar values.
8. Refresh /tags and confirm it remains.
9. Toggle published/sidebar and refresh again.
10. Open /api/internal/catalog/tags and confirm the tag exists with metadataJson.
11. Open /api/internal/platform/module-audit and confirm build v187.
