# v210 Product Artwork Rules Foundation

This build makes artwork requirements a first-class part of product setup.

Business cards can support upload or design from template, PDF requirements, front/back files and size matching.
Booklets can support cover + inner files or multi-page PDF setup.
Banners can support PDF/JPG/PNG with lower DPI and selected-size matching.
Boards and custom-cut products can require a cutline layer such as CutContour.

Pricing is still not implemented here. These fields exist so the future pricing/artwork engine can trust product setup.
